# G-05 · Name one real gap, and say what you would do about it

**Every application has a gap.** The reader will find it. You choose whether they find it from
you, framed, or from the CV, unframed.

## The shape

One sentence naming it. One sentence on what you would learn or how you would close it. One
sentence on what you bring that the people who have that skill usually do not.

*Example.* "I have not run a team of this size before. I have run one of five, and the part I
would need to learn is how much of the coordination has to become written rather than spoken.
What I bring in exchange is that I have been on the receiving end of that transition twice and
know which parts of it people resent."

## Rules for doing it well

**Pick one.** Listing three gaps is not honesty, it is an argument against yourself.

**Pick a real one.** A disguised strength ("I care too much about quality") reads as evasion
and costs more than the gap would have.

**Never apologise.** "Unfortunately I lack..." invites agreement. State it and move.

**Frame it as a direction, not a deficit.** "Deepen" rather than "gap". You are describing
where you are going, not what is missing.

## When not to do this

If the gap is the single central requirement of the role, naming it does not save the
application. **Consider whether it is the right role**, rather than how to phrase the problem.
