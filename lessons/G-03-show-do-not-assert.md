# G-03 · Replace every self-assessment with a checkable fact

**A reader cannot verify an adjective.** "Strong communicator" is a claim about you made by
you. A sentence describing something you did is evidence they can weigh.

## The substitution table

| Delete | Replace with |
|---|---|
| passionate about, deeply committed to | why you chose it, in one concrete clause |
| strong / excellent / extensive experience in | how long, at what scale, with what result |
| proven track record | the specific thing that was proven, and to whom |
| results-driven, detail-oriented, team player | one occasion where it was true |
| significantly improved, greatly enhanced | the number, or the before and after |
| cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, innovative | what it does that the previous thing did not |
| various, several, a range of, numerous | the actual count |
| responsible for | what you did, in a verb you can defend |
| helped to, contributed to, was involved in | your own part, stated plainly |
| leveraged, utilised, spearheaded | used, led |

## The rule underneath

**Every adjective about yourself is a sentence you have not written yet.** When you cut one,
something specific has to take its place, and that specific thing is the part worth reading.

## A caution about register

Some of these words are normal in some languages and some professions and are not a defect
there. The test is not the word, it is whether a stranger could check the claim. **If they
cannot, replace it, whatever language you are writing in.**
