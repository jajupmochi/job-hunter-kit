# G-06 · Read what the title screens for, and lead with that kind of evidence

**The same person is a strong candidate or a weak one depending on which evidence they lead
with.** The title tells you which.

## Two kinds of screen

Some titles screen for **delivery**: did you ship it, does it run, who used it, what broke.
Engineer, developer, manager, coordinator, operator, practitioner.

Some titles screen for **depth**: do you understand why, can you generalise, what did you
originate. Scientist, researcher, analyst, specialist, consultant, lead.

**Most people have both kinds of evidence and lead with the wrong one**, because they lead
with what they are proudest of rather than with what is being screened for.

## Reading the title

The words in the advert say it. "Build", "own", "deliver", "on call", "ship" is delivery.
"Investigate", "design", "publish", "advise", "define" is depth. When the advert mixes both,
the ORDER of the requirements usually tells you which one decides.

**A senior title is often a third thing: judgement.** What did you decide, what did you decide
against, and what happened. Neither shipping nor knowing, but choosing.

## What to do about it

Lead with the matching kind. Keep the other kind, one line, later. **Never devalue your own
answer before the reader has judged it** ("this may not be what you are looking for"), because
they will take you at your word.
