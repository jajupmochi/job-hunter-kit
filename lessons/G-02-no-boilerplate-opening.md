# G-02 · Do not spend the first sentence on nothing

**The first sentence is the one most likely to be read. Never spend it announcing that you are
applying.** The reader already knows.

## Openings to delete

- "I am writing to express my interest in the position of ..."
- "I am excited to apply for ..."
- "With great interest I read your advertisement for ..."
- "Please accept this letter as my application for ..."
- "I would like to take this opportunity to ..."

Each one uses a whole sentence to say what the subject line already said.

## What goes there instead

Whatever your strongest specific claim is. The thing you would say if you had one sentence.

*Before:* "I am writing to express my interest in the Data Analyst position advertised on your
website."

*After:* "Your posting asks for someone who can explain a model to a room that does not trust
it, and that is most of what I did for three years."

## One exception, and it is real

**If your own natural register is formal, do not let this rule flatten your voice into
somebody else's.** A formal opening that is specific still beats a casual opening that is
generic. The rule is against emptiness, not against formality.
