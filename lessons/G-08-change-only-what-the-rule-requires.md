# G-08 · When revising, change only what the rule requires

**A revision that improves a sentence you were not asked to touch is not an improvement, it is
a substitution of your voice for theirs.**

## The rule

When a specific fix is requested, fix that. Leave the surrounding sentences exactly as they
are, including the ones you would have written differently.

This matters most when you are helping someone else with their own writing, and it matters
just as much when an AI assistant is helping you. **The most common complaint about AI editing
is not that it is wrong, it is that everything comes back smoother and less like the person.**

## Reuse their exact phrasing

If a phrase already exists in the person's own drafts, in their own words, **reuse it verbatim
rather than improving it.** Their version carries their register. Yours carries a generic one.

## When restructuring IS the right answer

Sometimes it is. Then say so, and do it as an announced separate step: "the paragraph order is
working against you, may I restructure it?" **The problem is never restructuring. It is
restructuring silently while claiming to have fixed a typo.**

## The check

After a revision, diff it. **Every changed line should be explainable by the request.** Lines
you cannot account for are lines to put back.
