# Mode · critic

**Runs before anything leaves your hands.** A separate pass, not part of drafting, because the
person who wrote it cannot see it.

## Hard fails

Any one of these stops the send.

1. **A claim you cannot trace to a source.** [G-07](../lessons/G-07-every-claim-has-a-source.md)
2. **A number not re-checked today.**
3. **It survives the swap test.** [G-01](../lessons/G-01-anchor-it-in-them.md)
4. **An eligibility clause not read.** [G-12](../lessons/G-12-read-the-eligibility-clause-first.md)
5. **A third party named without a reason.**
6. **A rewritten sentence the applicant did not ask to have rewritten.**
   [G-08](../lessons/G-08-change-only-what-the-rule-requires.md)

## Then read for

- **Voice.** Does it sound like them? Read it aloud.
  [APPLICATION_VOICE_AND_STYLE.md](../docs/APPLICATION_VOICE_AND_STYLE.md)
- **The generated shapes.** Trailing clauses, appositives, triads, summary paragraphs.
- **Structure.** Them, you against their list, the shared problem, one gap, a conversation.
- **Length.** What could go without loss? Usually the last paragraph.

## Verify from the artifact

**Check the rendered document, not the source.** Check the built file, not the log. A process
that reports success can have written nothing.
[G-14](../lessons/G-14-look-before-you-write.md)

## Output

A list of what must change and what could. **Then the applicant decides**, and the applicant
sends.
