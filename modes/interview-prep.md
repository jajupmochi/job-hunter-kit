# Mode · interview-prep

## Gather

**The employer.** What they do, in your own words, well enough to say it in two sentences.
Recent public news. How they make money, or where the funding comes from.

**The team.** Who you will meet, what they work on, anything they have published or shipped
that you can honestly say something about. **Do not fabricate familiarity** with work you have
not read.

**The role.** Read the advert again. What is the problem they are hiring to solve? What would
success look like at six months?

## Prepare

**Your evidence, matched to their list.** For each requirement, the specific thing you would
describe, with the numbers, the difficulty, and what you would do differently now.

**The gap question.** They will ask about the weakest part of your fit. Have the honest answer
ready. [G-05](../lessons/G-05-name-the-gap.md)

**Your questions.** Three that show you thought about the work, one about how they judge
success in the role, and one about what happens next.

**Ask about next steps.** People forget, then worry that forgetting looked like indifference.

## Rules

- **Do not invent knowledge of their work.** One follow-up question exposes it, and it is
  unrecoverable.
- **Do not devalue your own answer** before they have judged it.
  [G-06](../lessons/G-06-match-evidence-to-the-title.md)
- **The best answer to a question you cannot answer** is what you do know, what you do not, and
  how you would find out. Experienced interviewers respect it and inexperienced ones cannot
  distinguish it from confidence.
