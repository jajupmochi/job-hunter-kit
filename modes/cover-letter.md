# Mode · cover-letter

## Before

[`_profile.md`](_profile.md) for voice and evidence, the advert itself, and the notes from
[`evaluate.md`](evaluate.md). **Read the advert again**, not your summary of it.

## Structure

Follow [G-04](../lessons/G-04-three-part-body-conversation-close.md):

1. **Them.** Something only this employer would recognise.
   [G-01](../lessons/G-01-anchor-it-in-them.md)
2. **You, against their list, in their order.** Evidence per requirement.
   [G-03](../lessons/G-03-show-do-not-assert.md)
3. **The shared problem.** One paragraph on a real difficulty in the work.
4. **One gap**, named and paired with what you would do about it.
   [G-05](../lessons/G-05-name-the-gap.md)
5. **Close by asking for a conversation.**

## Rules

- **Lead with the evidence the title screens for.**
  [G-06](../lessons/G-06-match-evidence-to-the-title.md)
- **Every factual claim traces to something.** Numbers re-checked today, not remembered.
  [G-07](../lessons/G-07-every-claim-has-a-source.md)
- **Keep their words.** Reuse the applicant's own phrasing verbatim where it exists.
  [G-08](../lessons/G-08-change-only-what-the-rule-requires.md)
- **No summary paragraph.** No opening that announces the application.
  [G-02](../lessons/G-02-no-boilerplate-opening.md)

## Before it goes out

**Run the swap test.** Replace the employer's name with a competitor's. If it still reads, it
is not finished.

Then [`critic.md`](critic.md). Then you send it.
